Samuel Reid Navarro was born in 1997 in Almere, the Netherlands. He studied music education at the Waldorf Institute in Leiden and choral conducting at the HKU in Utrecht, and earned his master’s in early music theory and composition at the Schola Cantorum Basiliensis under Johannes Menke.

He now teaches the theory of early music at the Kunstuniversität Graz. In Basel, he continues his singing studies in the medieval department of the Schola Cantorum, focusing on Renaissance and medieval polyphony and improvisation.
