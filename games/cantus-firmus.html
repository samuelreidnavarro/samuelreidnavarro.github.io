      How to enter and edit

      Pick a note value, then left-click on the staff where the note should go: between two notes inserts it there, after the last note adds it at the end. A left-click on a rest puts a note in its place. Right-click a note to turn it into a rest of the same value; right-click a rest to remove it. Left-click a note to select it (Play and the space bar then start from it); drag it up or down to change its pitch. Click the clef to change it, or the faint B flat beside it to add one.

      With a note selected, the value, rest and accidental buttons change that note. With nothing selected, they apply to the next note you enter. On a touch screen, tap a note to select it. Tap an empty place on the staff to put a note down there; the Undo, Redo and Delete buttons are at the top. Press and hold a note to turn it into a rest, or to remove a rest. Scrolling the page with a finger on the score does nothing to it.
