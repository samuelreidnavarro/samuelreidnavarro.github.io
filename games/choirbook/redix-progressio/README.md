These .krn files are copies of scores from the Josquin Research Project
(https://josquin.stanford.edu, https://github.com/josquin-research-project/jrp-scores)
and the 1520s Project by Benjamin Ory (https://1520s-project.org,
https://github.com/benory/1520s-project-scores), as collected by ReDiX
(Ugo Bindini, https://github.com/ugobindini/redix). They are kept here because
the currently published versions have been renamed or re-edited since, and the
bar numbers of the ReDiX search must match. Licence: CC BY-NC 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). All other pieces in the
Choir book library are loaded directly from those repositories.
