Acknowledgments

ChoirBook is built on the shoulders of giants. The app, itself ‘vibe-coded’ with the use of Anthropic’s Claude models, combines several concepts from different apps and websites.

The conception of having an online player of mensural music and plainchant with a hand beating the tactus predates ChoirBook and can be found in Contrapunto.ch.

Certain key commands and the idea of having a grid for note editing come from Dorico, created by Steinberg.

The fantastic organ sounds are made by Joseph Basquin, recorded at Romanswiller (Alsace). jeuxdorgues.com

On my website I link to several repositories of mensural music, transcribed by real humans as part of different projects. ChoirBook only contains the catalogue files, with each file being downloaded directly from the respective projects’ servers when requested.

Many thanks go out to the humans who spent hours transcribing these pieces from the original sources, an immense and specialized effort.
