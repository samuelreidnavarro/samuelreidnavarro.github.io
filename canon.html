This “rule of canons” gives the melodic motions allowed when improvising or writing a canon.

The large numbers are the vertical intervals of imitation: 4’ is a canon at the fourth above, and 5, 5, is a three voice canon at a fifth below, and then another one following the comes a fifth below too.

The small numbers are the melodic intervals the Dux may sing at each regular time interval, which is also the horizontal interval of imitation; the top row gives the intervals upward, the bottom row the intervals downward. At 5’, for instance, the Dux can stay on the same note, singing the unison (1), move up a 3rd, 5th, or down a 2nd, 4th, 6th and 8ve.

The red markings are forbidden melodic motions, that would create parallel perfect intervals. Jumps including two mi's or two fa's would in turn lead to tritones.
